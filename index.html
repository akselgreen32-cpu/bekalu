The Art of
Bespoke & Storytelling

I am Bekalu, the hands and vision behind the ALAS brand. My journey began with a profound respect for the heritage of tailoring—the meticulous measurement, the drape of fine wool, the perfect silhouette.

But clothing does not exist in a vacuum. It is meant to be seen, experienced, and shared. As a content creator, I bridge the gap between traditional craftsmanship and modern media, documenting the process, styling the final pieces, and curating a lifestyle that speaks to the modern sartorialist.

ALAS is not just a label; it is a narrative woven into every garment.
